Amamy · Cross-border deliveryGermany · Japan · Australia · US · 2024

Send in pairs, double the rewards.

Growing a cross-border brand where its customers live.
Challenge

Reach Vietnamese customers spread across Germany, Japan, Australia and the US with one small team.

Insight

Students abroad gather around embassy-backed associations — that is where the brand had to show up.

Idea

Partner with the biggest student communities, localize the feed by country, and pay both sides of every referral.

Execution

Embassy-backed events in Germany and Japan, VYSA vouchers, 200+ country-fit posts, a 5€ + 5€ referral.

Melius · Social businessHEC Montréal · 2023

From nutrition anxiety to guided action.

Insight

Execution
