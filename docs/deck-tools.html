What I built

Three skills. One engine. Every deck editable.

01

Deck Builder

/arie-deck-builder
  • 47 layouts and 13 themes, built from brand books
  • Morph transitions: charts and photos move between slides instead of jumping
  • Edit in place: text style bar, photo crop and replace, Ctrl+Z, comments
  • Export to PDF and editable PPTX
02

Dataviz

/arie-dataviz
  • Learned from BCG insight decks: action titles, exact survey questions, source lines
  • 12 consulting charts: 100% stacked, waterfall, slope, dumbbell, marimekko, 2×2
  • Delta callouts and takeaway bands that make the number land
03

Report Charts

/arie-report-charts
  • Quarterly and monthly reports: KPI rows with deltas vs last period
  • Heatmap, radar, funnel, dual axis, small multiples, event-marked timelines
  • A “how to read” line under every chart and an IMPLICATION box on every slide
  • One command turns the report into an editable deck

Team workflow

From brief to boardroom, without the slide grind.

01Brief in, draft out

The skill sorts the content into numbers, lists, plans and proof, then picks the layout. Long content is split, never shrunk.

02Review in place

Publish as a private page. Teammates fix text, swap photos and comment on the slide itself.

03Ship

PDF for email, PPTX for PowerPoint or Google Slides, or share the live link.
