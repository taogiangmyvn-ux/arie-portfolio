The problem

Monthly reports took too long — and AI decks made them worse.

Generic AI slide tools produce topic titles, buzzword bullets and stock icons. Leadership reads the title, finds no finding, and asks for a rewrite. The work was never the design; it was turning numbers into a decision.

Typical AI deck

“Q3 Report Overview 🚀” · five bullets about synergies · no numbers · no next step.

These skills

“NMV up 93% — one marketplace carries 85% of it.” · KPI row with deltas · one chart that proves it · implication and action in bold.

Team workflow

From brief to boardroom, without the slide grind.

  1. Brief in, draft out. The skill classifies each piece of content (numbers, lists, plans, proof) and picks the layout. Long content is split, never shrunk.
  2. Review in place. The deck is published as a private page; teammates fix text, swap photos and leave comments on the slide itself.
  3. Ship. PDF for email, PPTX for PowerPoint or Google Slides, or the live link. Brand colours, fonts and logo rules come from the brand book.
