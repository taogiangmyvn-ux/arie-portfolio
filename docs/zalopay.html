Follow the passport.

Vietnam's wallet market stopped growing. So ZaloPay stopped chasing new users and followed the ones it already had — out of the country. A teardown of the smartest move in Vietnamese fintech marketing, plus the media plan and growth plan I'd run next.

Slide 01 · The strategic read

Same user. New occasion.

Vietnam's e-wallet market is not just saturated — it is contracting. Active wallets nationwide fell from 36.2M (end-2023) to 30.3M (Sep 2025) while payment volume boomed, because bank apps and VietQR made basic payments free for everyone.2,3 In that market, buying a new user is the most expensive thing you can do.

The losing game

Fight MoMo, ShopeePay, VNPay and every bank app for the same shrinking pool of Vietnamese wallet users. Compete on cashback. Burn budget on installs that scan one QR and go quiet.

→
The move ZaloPay made

Don't find a new user. Find a new occasion for the user you already have. The 4M+ trips Vietnamese people take abroad each half-year are moments where cash and cards actively hurt — and where nobody had built the habit yet.

Demand was already moving

Outbound travel is running well above pre-COVID levels: 4.06M trips in H1 2025, +54% YoY. Japan alone took a record 678,500 Vietnamese visitors in 2025.1,10

The category was empty

Vietnamese travellers abroad had two bad options: exchange cash before flying, or eat a card FX markup. No wallet owned "pay abroad" in the traveller's head.

It fits the rebrand

ZaloPay dropped "e-wallet" for "payment platform" in July 2024 — "Làm mới mọi trải nghiệm về tiền". Cross-border is the proof of that promise, not a side feature.7,8

The insight is not "go international". It is: when you cannot add users, add occasions.

Slide 02 · The friction it removes

The trip is paid for. The paying is the annoying part.

Cards carry a 2–4% foreign-transaction and conversion markup. ZaloPay's QR route runs "không phát sinh phí và áp dụng tỷ giá cạnh tranh" — no fee, competitive rate — and shows the rate before you confirm.4,5

Bar widths are illustrative of the fee ranges cited, not a measured basket.

Cash means planning to be wrong

You guess the amount before you fly, queue at a counter, carry it, and come home with leftover notes you exchange back at a loss. The friction is upfront and irreversible.

Cards fail exactly where you spend

Street stalls, night markets, small cafés and local transit — the places that define a trip — are the places a Visa terminal isn't. Asia solved that with QR, not plastic.

QR is already the local default

China, Japan, Korea, Singapore and Malaysia all run national or near-universal QR schemes. The traveller doesn't need a new behaviour — they need their own app to speak the local QR.

Slide 03 · What actually shipped

Two directions, zero new behaviour.

The whole feature is deliberately unremarkable to use, which is the point. Nothing about it asks the traveller to learn anything: it is the same "Quét QR" button they already press in Vietnam, with a country picker added.

Markets in-app
Singapore · Korea · Malaysia · China

Japan and Indonesia also reported in coverage and third-party guides; ZaloPay says more markets are coming.4,5

Rails it rides
Alipay+ · DuitNow QR

Both logos appear in ZaloPay's own product screen. Third-party guides also map NETS/SGQR in Singapore, ZeroPay in Korea, QRIS in Indonesia.5

FX handling
Shown before confirm

Amount in local currency, the rate, and the VND total on one screen. Transparency is the feature — it removes the "what did that cost me?" anxiety cards create.

Wallet balance pays
Existing VND balance

No foreign-currency account, no new card, no separate app. The user's existing balance and KYC do the work.4,5

Read as a growth decision, this is a zero-learning-curve feature attached to a high-anxiety moment. That combination is rare and it is why the campaign could lean entirely on demonstration rather than explanation.

Slide 04 · Why it was possible at all

ZaloPay didn't build a network. It plugged into one.

Signing overseas merchants one by one would take a decade and a balance sheet ZaloPay does not have — VNG's fintech arm was still loss-making in 2025, with breakeven targeted for end-2026.9 Joining an existing cross-border rail converts that decade into an integration.

What it buys

Overnight acceptance at the merchant density a wallet could never sign alone — and the network keeps growing without ZaloPay spending anything. Ant International now reports 150M+ merchants across 100+ markets.6

What it doesn't buy

Exclusivity. GCash, TrueMoney, TNG and DANA ride the same rail, and Vietnamese banks are wiring up bilateral QR too. The feature is copyable; the brand association is not. That is exactly why the marketing mattered more than the integration.

Slide 05 · The brand decision

They bought trust, not reach.

For the April 2026 push, ZaloPay made Khoai Lang Thang (Đinh Võ Hoài Phương) the face of paying abroad.11,12 On a reach basis he is not Vietnam's biggest creator. On a believability basis for this specific product, he is close to the only right answer.

01

His genre is the payment moment

He built a decade of work on street food, local markets and small vendors across Asia. Those are precisely the counters where a card fails and a QR works. The demo doesn't need a script — it's his normal footage.

02

Trust is the actual conversion lever

Paying abroad with an unfamiliar method is a fear purchase: will my money work in a country where I can't argue? That objection is answered by a person the audience already believes, not by a media buy. He won Content Creator of the Year at Vietnam iContent 2025, co-run by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and VnExpress.13

03

The persona matches the price point

"Khoai lang thang" means a wandering sweet potato — deliberately humble, unbranded, mass-accessible. It maps onto a product used for a 12-ringgit coffee, not a luxury purchase. A glossier celebrity would have made the feature feel expensive.

Reach tells people a feature exists. Trust is what makes them try it 3,000km from home.

Slide 06 · Campaign anatomy

Every choice in it is an activation choice.

Coverage of the China trip ran on 29 April 2026 — the eve of Vietnam's 30/4–1/5 holiday, the single biggest outbound travel window of the first half of the year. Nothing about the mechanics is accidental.11,12

The proof point
"Từ trung tâm thương mại đến gánh hàng rong"

From shopping malls to street carts. They led with the hardest case, killing the obvious objection that it only works at big chains.

The offer
Code KHOAIZLP · 50% off, max 50,000đ

Minimum spend 10,000đ, two uses. Tiny by design — this is not a discount play.

The gate
First-time international QR users only

The incentive is aimed exactly at the behaviour that's missing: the first foreign scan. It buys a habit, not volume.

The timing
Live through 30 April

Published into peak trip-planning and departure, not into a quiet week. Offer expiry matches the travel window.

What I'd copy

The first-use gate. Most wallet promos pay everyone for transactions they'd make anyway. Capping the reward at 50,000đ and restricting it to first-time cross-border users means the money goes to changing behaviour, not subsidising it. Indonesia already ran the counter-experiment: 71% of e-wallet users there were acquired by promotions, but only 23% stayed for them.14

What looks thin

It reads as a holiday burst, not a system. One creator, one destination, one PR wave, one expiring code. Travel is a repeating four-stage journey and the campaign only shows up at stage three. No visible pre-trip capture, no post-trip retention hook, no second destination. That gap is what the next two slides fix.

Slide 07 · The media plan I'd run

Meet the traveller four times, not once.

A trip is a funnel with dates on it. The install decision happens weeks before departure, the habit forms in-destination, and the business value lands after the plane home. One plan, four moments, each with its own job and its own metric.

Budget shape

Weight toward Plan and In-destination, where intent is highest and the behaviour is actually set. Dream is earned-first: creator content, not paid reach.

Creator portfolio

Keep one anchor of Khoai's calibre for trust, then add destination-specialist micro-creators — Japan, Korea, Thailand — so each market gets a local proof rather than one national campaign.

Always-on beats bursts

Travel demand has a calendar: Tết, 30/4, summer, year-end. A burst wins one holiday; a seasonal always-on plan compounds across all four.

Slide 08 · The growth plan

The trip is the wedge. Home is the business.

Paying abroad is a brilliant activation story and a poor revenue story on its own — a handful of trips a year cannot carry a P&L. Its real value is that it creates a high-trust moment the wallet can convert into everyday domestic use, which is where frequency and margin live.

Stage shapes are illustrative of where the leaks sit, not published data — ZaloPay discloses none of these rates. The point is the sequence: the campaign optimises stage 4, but stages 3 and 5 decide whether it pays.

Three experiments, in order
01Pre-trip triggerPre-trip activation rate

Detect trip intent already visible in-app — a flight or hotel booking, an eSIM purchase, an FX search — and fire a one-tap "turn on paying in [country]" flow. Hypothesis: travellers activated before departure scan abroad at materially higher rates than those who discover the feature at a counter. Guardrail: no notification to users without trip signals.

02First-scan conciergeFirst scan within 24h

On first connection to a foreign network, surface three nearby accepting merchants and the live rate. Hypothesis: the blocker at the counter isn't trust in the app, it's not knowing which counters accept it. Show acceptance, remove the guess. Guardrail: location permission opt-in, no dark patterns.

03Homecoming loopD30 domestic retention

On return, deliver a trip spend recap — what they paid, the rate they got, what a card would have cost — and pair it with one domestic habit offer. Hypothesis: a user shown their own saved money converts to a second habit better than a generic promo. This is where the wedge either compounds or ends.

North star

Share of trips paid with ZaloPay

Not installs, not campaign reach, not cumulative users. One number that only improves if travellers actually pay — measurable per destination, per season, per creator cohort.

Trailing twin

D30 domestic retention of cross-border users

The honesty check. If this doesn't move, cross-border is an expensive brand campaign wearing a product costume.

Slide 09 · What I'd watch

Three ways this stops working.

The feature gets commoditised

The same rail carries GCash, TrueMoney, TNG and DANA, and Vietnamese banks are building bilateral QR links. Mitigation: race to own the occasion in memory — the traveller's default — while the capability is still novel. Brand association outlives feature parity.

Seasonality masquerades as growth

Holiday spikes will look like traction. Mitigation: judge every campaign on same-season comparisons and on D30 domestic retention, never on peak-week transaction counts.

One trip, then silence

A user who scans in Osaka and never opens the app in Ho Chi Minh City is a cost, not a win. Mitigation: the homecoming loop is not optional — it's the experiment that decides whether the whole play has a return.

A wallet that travels with you earns a place on the home screen. That place is the asset — not the trip.
