Independent brand strategy case · 2025–2030 horizon

Everywhere is not a reason.

TGDĐ won twenty years by being the closest store in Vietnam. Online made proximity free — and the record 2025 was bought with cost discipline, not preference. This is a relevance plan built on the one claim rivals cannot fund.

Slide 01 · The reframe

They fixed the P&L. They did not fix the brand.

The obvious read is decline: a price war, collapsing margin, hundreds of stores shut. Check the tape and the opposite is true — 2025 was MWG's best year ever: revenue 156,166 tỷ (+16%), profit around 7,000 tỷ, TGDĐ + TopZone up 18%.1,2 Any strategy that opens with "TGDĐ is losing" is answering a question that closed in 2024.

What recovered What did not

Bar heights are illustrative of direction, not a common scale — operating metrics are published1,2,3; "price perception" has no public index, and that absence is itself the point.

01The recovery was operational

Close the weak stores, tighten logistics, hold discipline. 2025 H1 grew 12% with roughly 200 fewer stores than a year earlier — proof the "fewer but better" call was right.3

02None of it answered the brand question

Nothing in a store-closure programme changes why a shopper picks TGDĐ over CellphoneS. The forums still say the same thing they said in 2023: "TGDĐ đắt hơn 5–10%".5

03And the lever is nearly spent

Efficiency is a one-time gain by construction. The next five years need a reason to be chosen, not a leaner cost base. That is a brand problem, and it is still open.

Slide 02 · Diagnosis

Three things converged. Two did not.

"Buying anywhere is the same" is not a perception problem to be corrected. On three dimensions it is simply true — and a strategy has to concede that before it can find the ground that is still contestable.

Converged · 01
Product

Every authorised chain sells the identical SKU with the identical manufacturer warranty. An iPhone is an iPhone. Assortment breadth stopped being a reason to travel.

Converged · 02
Price

The 2023 "Giá rẻ quá" campaign proved anyone can match — rivals copied the slogan within weeks.4 Worse, it taught the market that TGDĐ's prices were negotiable all along.

Converged · 03
Availability

Shoppers research online for hours, then spend minutes collecting the device.6 Being 3km closer is worth nothing once the phone in their hand is the nearest store.

What did not converge

Risk absorption — who guarantees it is genuine, who fixes it in year two, what it is worth when you replace it. And accountable presence at national scale — 63/63 provinces of staffed, answerable service. Marketplaces structurally cannot promise the first; small chains cannot afford the second.

Which exposes the real finding: TGDĐ already funds this and has never charged for it. The brief's own explanation of the 5–10% gap is that TGDĐ spends on warranty, after-sales and returns while rivals spend on flash sales. That gap is not a pricing error. It is an unmarketed product.5

The strategic problem in one line: TGDĐ still wins on reach, and no longer on reason.

Slide 03 · Repositioning Brief Q1

Stop selling the box. Sell the ownership.

Three segments buy technology in Vietnam, and TGDĐ has been trying to serve all three at once. A choice has to be made — including the choice of who to stop chasing.

Cede it
Price-first

Wants the lowest number on the screen, will drive across town for 300k. Unwinnable without becoming Hoàng Hà — and the 2023 price war already proved what that costs.

Owned by Hoàng Hà Mobile
Compete selectively
Tech-first

Early adopters, Apple ecosystem, forum natives. Loyal to community and expertise, which a mass chain cannot fake — but TopZone can hold the premium end credibly.

Owned by CellphoneS
Own it
Risk-first

Spending a meaningful share of a month's income on a device the household depends on daily. Fears a fake, a dead unit in year two, a worthless trade-in. The largest of the three and the least served.

Owned by nobody — yet

For that buyer the downside — a fake, a dead unit in month 14, a worthless trade-in — costs far more than a 5% saving. Nobody owns that fear, and TGDĐ is the only chain already built to remove it.

Positioning statement

For Vietnamese buyers making a significant, daily-dependency technology purchase, thegioididong is the retailer that makes owning it risk-free — because it is the only chain that pairs nationwide accountable service with authorised authenticity, a guaranteed resale value, and a commitment that lasts the life of the device.

Functional benefit

Guaranteed genuine. Service in every province. No-fuss returns. A resale value committed in writing at purchase.

Emotional benefit

An tâm — the confidence to buy the better thing and stop thinking about it. The brand idea: "Mua an tâm, dùng an tâm."

Reason to believe

63/63 provinces since 2012 and roughly 2,000 stores.7 Authorised reseller status via TopZone. And the honest one: the 5–10% premium is what funds it.

Do not erase the price gap. Explain it.

Slide 04 · Pricing & experience Brief Q3

Make the premium legible, not smaller.

The premium is real, funded and invisible — so it reads as greed. Four moves turn it into a priced product, none of which requires a discount.

01Name the premium on the tagPrice-tag as media

Print what the extra buys, in numbers a shopper can check: "Giá này gồm: đổi mới 30 ngày · bảo hành mở rộng 12 tháng · cam kết thu lại". Days, months and percentages — never adjectives.

02Guaranteed trade-in valueThe flagship mechanic

At purchase, commit in writing to a minimum buy-back in 24 months. It converts a premium into arithmetic: a 5% premium against a guaranteed residual is trivially worth paying. It also forces a return visit and pre-books the next upgrade. TGDĐ already runs thu cũ đổi mới and used-device resale — the infrastructure exists.8

03Match on the box, win on the wrapMargin defence

Honour a price match on an identical SKU when a customer asks — it kills the objection at the moment of truth. But never advertise price. Most shoppers never ask; the ones who do were leaving anyway.

04Retire the discount reflexFund the guarantee instead

On the 2025 TGDĐ + ĐMX revenue base of about 105,700 tỷ, one point of gross margin is worth roughly 1,057 tỷ đồng a year. The 2023 price war gave up 2.9 points.1,4 Redirect a fraction of that into underwriting the trade-in guarantee: same P&L line, but it builds an asset instead of renting a headline.

Slide 05 · Portfolio Brief Q4

Organised by category, it dilutes. Organised by role, it compounds.

The portfolio feels scattered because it is filed by what things are — phones, appliances, watches, services, Apple. Refile it by the job each one does for the brand, and the same catalogue becomes a system with a clear rule for what does not belong.

The rule that prevents dilution

Every group must earn its place by role, not by revenue. If a category cannot drive traffic, margin, trust or frequency, it belongs in another chain or in the app — not on the storefront and not in the brand's voice.

Slide 06 · The next growth driver Brief Q2

From footprint to frequency.

Retail brand growth has three inputs. Two are at their ceiling and one has barely been touched — which decides where the next five years of investment should go.

Customers · capped
~25% share, 63/63 provinces

Penetration is close to its ceiling and the store lever was just used: ~200 closed, revenue still up.3,9

×
Occasions · the opening
~0.4 → 3+ a year

A phone lasts 2–3 years, so purchase alone gives well under one occasion a year. Service, trade-in and accessories are the only way to add more.

×
Value per occasion · capped
Attach & mix

Improvable at the margin, but bounded by what a household will spend on one device.

Occasion figures are modelled from a 2–3 year replacement cycle plus one trade-in valuation, one accessory and one service touch a year. Directional, and exactly the number a brand team should measure first.

So the growth driver is

Service occasions per customer per year

Not stores, not share, not average basket. It is the only input with room left — and note that it is the same answer as the services row in the portfolio. Q2 and Q4 are one strategy seen from two sides.

Where the money goes — in order
1Service capacity & the trade-in guarantee

The moat itself. Repair throughput, refurbishment, residual-value underwriting.

2Device-lifecycle data

Know the age of every device sold. Month 20 is a trigger, not a coincidence.

3Own app as the frequency channel

Online for lifetime and data, stores for trust and experience — the split that JD's retail model makes explicit.10

×Not: more stores, not more discount

One is a spent lever; the other funds a claim the brand cannot own.

Slide 07 · Activation

Answer the objection where it is made.

The "TGDĐ đắt" belief does not live in mass media. It lives in forums and Facebook groups, in the exact places a shopper checks before buying.5 So the channel plan is inverted: earned first, owned second, paid last and narrow.

ChannelJobWhat runs thereMetric
EarnedBreak the price belief Take the guarantee to VNReview, Tinh Tế and the buying groups — as arithmetic, not as a slogan. A guaranteed residual is inherently argued about, which is the point Share of voice on "đắt/rẻ" threads
OwnedTurn buyers into returners Price tag as media; app lifecycle triggered on device age; staff script that quotes the guarantee before the price Trade-in guarantee take-up
PaidProve, don't shout Demonstration over celebrity: one honest film of a 24-month-old phone traded in at exactly the promised number, in a real store Assisted lift on service occasions
Retail floorWhere the claim is kept Service counter moved to the front of the store, not the back. The moat should be the first thing a shopper sees Service visits per store

Launch timing follows the objection, not the calendar. A promise about year two is not a Tết message.

Slide 08 · How I'd measure it, and what could break

One number on the wall.

North star

Occasions per customer per year

Revenue and footfall both flatter a discount. This one only rises if the brand built a reason to come back.

Trailing twin

Share of revenue from customers with 2+ occasions

The honesty check on whether frequency is turning into money rather than into activity.

Guardrails, always on
Gross margin floor — never below 18.5% againTrade-in residual cost vs planService NPSStaff attritionPremium mix

The guarantee is mispriced

Residual-value risk is the real exposure. Mitigation: launch only on models with liquid, predictable resale — iPhone and flagship Samsung — cap by tier, and price the guarantee off actual refurbishment data, not marketing ambition.

Rivals copy it

They will try. Mitigation: it needs resale scale and a balance sheet, which is exactly where a chain of ~2,000 stores and a cash-rich parent has the advantage. And unlike a discount, whoever is first keeps the association.

"An tâm" stays an adjective

Soft claims die on forums. Mitigation: every element of the promise must be a checkable number — 30 days, 12 months, a committed percentage. If it cannot be printed on a tag, it does not ship.

Twenty years were won on being closest. The next five have to be won on being trusted.

Let's talk

I check the premise before I answer the question.

The brief said a brand in trouble. The tape said a record year. Both were true, and the gap between them was the actual case — growth bought with efficiency, on a brand question still unanswered. That is the work I like: find the real problem, then make it costed and testable.
